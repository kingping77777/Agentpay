# 🎬 AgentPay — 3-Minute Video Demo & Screen Recording Script

> **Target Duration**: Exactly 3 Minutes (180 Seconds)  
> **Tone**: Confident, enthusiastic, and clear  
> **Preparation**: Open `http://127.0.0.1:5173/` (or your live deployed Vercel URL) in full screen before pressing record.

---

## ⏱️ Video Timeline & Script

---

### **Section 1: Hook & Platform Overview (0:00 – 0:30)**

- **On-Screen**: Full dashboard view showing the **Phaser 3 Virtual Agent Office** (left side) with animated pixel-art agents walking, and the **Command Center** (right side).
- **Action**: Hover mouse over the pixel agents in the office and point to the status bar at the bottom.
- **Voiceover**:
  > *"Hi everyone! Welcome to **AgentPay** — an autonomous multi-agent commerce platform designed to revolutionize digital shopping through collaborative AI agents.*
  >
  > *Instead of relying on a single generic chatbot, AgentPay orchestrates a specialized network of AI agents:*
  > *1. **Michael**, our Lead Sales Discovery Agent.*
  > *2. **TechStore**, our Merchant Inventory and pricing agent.*
  > *3. And the **Authority Gatekeeper**, guaranteeing 100% deterministic safety, fraud protection, and customer budget compliance."*

---

### **Section 2: Universal Product Search & Budget Filtering (0:30 – 1:15)**

- **On-Screen**: Command Center chat feed.
- **Action 1**: Click the chat input. Type: `show me shoes` and press **Enter** (or click Send).
- **Voiceover**:
  > *"Let's see it in action. I'll ask: 'show me shoes'. In real-time, our Universal Product Intelligence engine synthesizes authentic footwear options — fetching real market pricing in Indian Rupees, high-resolution product photography, and complete specifications for Puma and Nike running shoes."*

- **Action 2**: Point the cursor at the returned product cards with photos, specs, and prices (₹2,899 and ₹3,495).
- **Voiceover**:
  > *"Notice how comprehensive each card is: verified seller ratings, instant stock status, and key specs like EVA cushioning and breathable mesh."*

- **Action 3**: Type: `running shoes below 3000` and press **Enter**. Show that only the ₹2,899 Puma shoes appear.
- **Voiceover**:
  > *"AgentPay strictly respects customer budgets. When I query 'running shoes below 3000', our system applies real-time budget filtering, showing only products that fit the customer's financial ceiling."*

---

### **Section 3: Inter-Agent Collaboration (A2A Network) & Telemetry (1:15 – 2:00)**

- **On-Screen**: Scroll down to highlight the **INTER-AGENT DIALOGUE (A2A NETWORK)** box inside the message feed.
- **Action 1**: Highlight the A2A communication box showing Sales Agent ➔ Merchant Agent ➔ Authority Agent dialogue.
- **Voiceover**:
  > *"Here is what makes AgentPay truly revolutionary: our Inter-Agent Dialogue Network. When a query is made, the Sales Agent introduces customer intent, the Merchant Agent checks inventory and offers partner bundle discounts, and the Authority Agent verifies spending policies — completely visible to the user."*

- **Action 2**: Click the **A2A Graph**, **Telemetry**, and **Actions** tabs at the top of the Command Center.
- **Voiceover**:
  > *"Switching to our A2A Graph and Telemetry tabs, judges can inspect the live decision matrix, memory pools, and immutable audit logs tracking every single autonomous agent action."*

---

### **Section 4: 1-Click Direct Buy & Instant UPI QR Checkout (2:00 – 2:40)**

- **On-Screen**: Product cards in the chat feed.
- **Action 1**: Click the **"⚡ BUY DIRECT"** button on one of the shoe cards.
- **Voiceover**:
  > *"For frictionless shopping, we engineered 1-Click Direct Buy. Clicking 'BUY DIRECT' immediately opens our verified checkout modal with the customer's delivery address pre-filled."*

- **Action 2**: Click **"CONTINUE TO ONLINE PAYMENT"** to reveal the live **UPI QR code** and Razorpay payment flow.
- **Voiceover**:
  > *"Proceeding to payment, AgentPay generates an instant UPI QR code and Razorpay settlement trigger, allowing fast, verified checkout with zero hallucination risk."*

---

### **Section 5: Tech Stack & Closing (2:40 – 3:00)**

- **On-Screen**: Close the modal, return to the main dashboard showing the Phaser 3 office and bottom telemetry status.
- **Action**: Zoom in slightly or move mouse across the telemetry bar.
- **Voiceover**:
  > *"AgentPay is built on a robust, modern stack: **FastAPI, SQLAlchemy, and PostgreSQL** on the backend, **React 18, Vite, and Phaser 3** on the frontend, and **Google Gemini 2.0** for multi-agent intelligence.*
  >
  > *Thank you for watching, and welcome to the future of autonomous agentic commerce!"*

---

## 🎯 Pro Tips for Your Recording
1. **Screen Resolution**: Set display to **1080p (1920×1080)**.
2. **Smooth Clicks**: Pause 1 second before and after clicking buttons so viewers can easily track the cursor.
3. **Audio**: Speak clearly with steady enthusiasm.
